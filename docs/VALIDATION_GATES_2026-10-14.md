# Evidence gates for Crypto AI Trading Lab

* No real-money trading or API credentials.
* Reproduce Python tests on a clean checkout.
* Use dated, checksummed, independently acquired BTC/ETH OHLCV data.
* Compare to cash, buy-and-hold, and exposure-matched portfolios with identical fee and slippage assumptions.
* Prefer pre-registered models on new periods; earlier BTC history has already influenced strategy selection and is exploratory only.
* Validate missing candles, stale quotes, market gaps, and operational restarts.
* Record cost stress, worst-regime loss, and median relative fold return; do not claim profitability based on a single positive backtest.
* Do not connect any live trading account without separate authorization and legal due diligence.
